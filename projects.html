Spatial Computing Artificial Intelligence Product Strategy GTM

MindSpace: AI-Powered Mixed Reality Wellness Ecosystem

V1.0 Product Architecture · Safety Framework · Go-To-Market Strategy

Role Product Manager (AI & XR)
Focus Spatial Computing, AI, PRD, GTM, Regulatory Risk

Figure 2.1 — MindSpace V1.0 System Architecture and User Flow. Illustrates the end-to-end user journey across Mixed Reality and mobile platforms, including onboarding, wellness sessions, cloud sync, and engagement tracking.

Project Summary

Conceptualized the end-to-end V1.0 product architecture, safety framework, and go-to-market strategy for an AI-powered Mixed Reality wellness platform. The solution was designed as a scalable, non-clinical B2B2C ecosystem focused on preventive wellness, user engagement, and long-term retention.

Business Strategy & Product Outcomes

Regulatory Risk Management

Positioned the product strictly within the preventive wellness space by excluding clinical therapy functions, predictive diagnostics, and medical decision-making capabilities. This approach reduced regulatory complexity, accelerated time-to-market, and avoided medical device certification requirements.

User Engagement Strategy

Addressed declining engagement — a common challenge in mindfulness apps — through immersive Mixed Reality environments powered by Meta Quest 3, spatial audio, and contextual personalization.

AI Safety Framework

Designed an AI Wellness Companion governed by predefined safety boundaries: harmful keyword detection, escalation pathways, and support resource recommendations to promote responsible AI interactions.

Product Success Metrics

1,000 Monthly Active Users within 6 months
8 min Average session duration target
65%+ Session completion rate
40% 30-day retention rate target

Cross-Functional Leadership

Defended and validated the Product Requirements Document through stakeholder review sessions involving engineering, business, and product trade-offs. Balanced technical feasibility, hardware constraints, and commercialization requirements throughout.

MVP Product Framework

Contextual Mood Check-In System

A mood assessment experience combining manual user input with optional voice sentiment analysis. User responses dynamically informed personalized wellness recommendations.

Mixed Reality Wellness Environments

Immersive environments — Beach Relaxation, Forest Focus Zone, Deep Focus Workspace — each incorporating adaptive lighting, spatial audio, and guided interactions.

Cross-Device Experience

Companion mobile application supporting mood tracking, behavioral logging, and retention-focused engagement mechanisms with continuity between mobile and MR experiences.

Technical & Operational Requirements

Environment startup time < 5 seconds
AI response latency < 2 seconds
Data encryption (at rest) AES-256
Data encryption (in transit) TLS 1.3
Consent management User-controlled, granular

Scope Boundaries

✓ In Scope

  • Preventive wellness experiences
  • Mood tracking & personalization
  • AI Wellness Companion (safety-bounded)
  • Mixed Reality environments
  • Mobile companion app
  • B2B2C onboarding

✗ Out of Scope

  • Clinical therapy functions
  • Predictive diagnostics
  • Medical decision-making
  • Medical device certification

Project Summary

Redesigned the Fitbit Mobile Application (v4.49) using a data-driven User-Centered Design approach, resulting in a 12.86% increase in overall usability. The project focused on identifying and eliminating key friction points affecting user engagement, onboarding efficiency, and long-term retention.

The goal was to transform the experience from a passive health-tracking tool into a more intuitive and engaging lifestyle companion — reducing cognitive load and converting passive data into perceived value.
