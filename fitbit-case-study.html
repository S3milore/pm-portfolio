Role
PM & Lead Researcher
Industry
Health & Fitness Tech
Objective
Retention Optimization
Key Outcome
+12.86% Usability

Executive Summary

Redesigned the Fitbit Mobile Application (v4.49) using a data-driven User-Centered Design approach, resulting in a 12.86% increase in overall usability. The project focused on identifying and eliminating key friction points affecting user engagement, onboarding efficiency, and long-term retention.

Streamlined Core Dashboard

The home dashboard was redesigned to prioritize the most important user metrics, including daily steps and calories burned. This reduced information overload and improved visibility of key performance indicators.

Gamified Reward System

Personalized User Experience

Dark Mode Implementation

Improved Error and Offline States

Market Validation

An important indicator of product thinking is the ability to identify and prioritize emerging user needs before they become industry standards. In August 2025, Fitbit introduced native Dark Mode support, aligning perfectly with our research findings.
